      About RoonDeck
      
        
      
        
      
          
      Made for the love of music.
      
          
      RoonDeck started as a personal project. I wanted a dedicated way to enjoy and explore my music collection, without constantly reaching for a phone or opening an app.
      
          
      Something that brought album artwork, music discovery and the joy of listening back into focus. So I built one.
      
        
      
        
      
          
      A little screen. A lot of music.
      
          
      Built for Raspberry Pi, RoonDeck runs on Alpine Linux with a GTK4 touchscreen interface. It connects to your Roon Server to browse music and control your existing zones. A working Roon Server and subscription are required.
      
          
      Optional Roon Bridge turns the Pi into an audio endpoint; it is not needed to control playback elsewhere.
      
        
      
        
      
          
      Made to do more.
      
          
      • Enjoy album artwork, browse your library and explore music discovery.
      • Control playback and choose where you listen.
      • Make the display your own with themes, brightness and orientation settings.
      • Add optional Home Assistant controls and Netdata monitoring.
      
          
      Some library and discovery features use a reverse-engineered internal Roon interface. Future Roon updates may affect compatibility.
